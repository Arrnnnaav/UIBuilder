- A "$$$$"-level look: agency-grade, not a template (user, 2026-09-24).

This is a design preference; do not invent competition titles/results or expose private footage.

- The references they collected lean toward studio/editorial (Poch, Moah, Noth, Airborne), playful interaction (Bruno Simon, K95), and dark product storytelling (Lexspace, Brik).
- For every future site, the owner does not want a generic/static “normal” template. They want a distinctive, high-craft visual concept with a project-specific story and memorable motion/interaction. This applies across project types; trust, clarity, accessibility, reduced motion, mobile usability and performance remain mandatory. (owner, 2026-09-29)

## Avoid
- Generic purple/blue gradient SaaS heroes.
- Template-looking dashboards and Tailwind default styling.
- Heavy glow and random glassmorphism.
- Slow, decorative animation. Bouncing.
- Copying reference assets, fonts or copy.
