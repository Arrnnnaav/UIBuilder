## Wants
- A "$$$$"-level look: agency-grade, not a template (user, 2026-09-24).

## Avoid
- Generic purple/blue gradient SaaS heroes.
- Template-looking dashboards and Tailwind default styling.
- Heavy glow and random glassmorphism.
- Slow, decorative animation. Bouncing.
- Copying reference assets, fonts or copy.
