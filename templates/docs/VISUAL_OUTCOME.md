# Visual outcome review

The JSON companion records an owner's assessment of the exact reviewed artifact hashes and evidence. Rate each dimension from 1 (weak) to 5 (excellent). Link screenshots, offline review pages, and reports by repository-relative path or SHA-256 reference. Do not substitute a model's self-rating for owner review.

The validator checks record completeness and an average rating of at least 4; this is a diagnostic threshold only. It does not approve G2/G2.5/G3.5 or replace accessibility, responsive, performance, or usability gates.
