## Fit to the brief (facts, not decisions)
- **Evidence:** PlanetScale (methodology before result) and elu (a mono label over a large numeral, and an accessible animated number) are the two direct precedents for showing measured before→after results.
- **Tone:** noth, elu and PlanetScale all pair a grotesk or mono with a restrained palette. None uses a gradient hero or glass effects, which matches `brain/preferences.md` → Avoid.
- **Performance:** airborne, noth, elu and PlanetScale are DOM/CSS only; nothing in these mechanisms needs WebGL. k95 does, so it is listed only as an alternate.
