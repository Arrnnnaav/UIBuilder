# Design constraints: portfolio (constraints, not a look)
- Typography carries the identity. Use at most 2 families.
- The work is the hero, so UI chrome stays quieter than the images.
- Create a story-led, memorable experience with a clear motion arc and at least one signature interaction. Keep supporting motion purposeful and restrained; don't make the site feel like a generic static portfolio.
- Design mobile first: case studies must read well at 390px.
- From preferences, avoid: generic gradients, glow, template cards, bouncing motion.
