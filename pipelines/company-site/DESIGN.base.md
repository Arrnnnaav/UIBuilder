# Design constraints: company-site
- Trust comes first: clear hierarchy, real proof high on the page, and no gimmicks in the first viewport.
- If the client keeps their brand, the tokens come from it: convert the logo colours to oklch and check contrast. If the brand evolves, keep a recognisable link to the old one.
- A project-specific experience thesis and memorable motion arc. Include at least one signature moment (for example, an interactive product demonstration or a case-study transition); keep trust signals and core copy clear and immediately readable.
- Content-dense pages (services, blog) favour readability: a 65ch measure and generous line-height.
