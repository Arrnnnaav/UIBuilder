# Prompt snippets: company-site
- **Hero:** an outcome for the buyer, plus a proof chip ("Trusted by N …" or a metric). No category-speak ("We are a leading …").
- **Service page frame:**
  1. who it's for
  2. the problem
  3. what we do (3 steps)
  4. deliverables
  5. proof
  6. FAQ (answer-first)
  7. CTA
- **FAQ seeds per service:**
  - How much does SERVICE cost? (give a range only if the client confirms it)
  - How long does it take?
  - What do you need from me?
  - Do you work with SEGMENT?
